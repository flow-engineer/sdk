Flow Messaging, live mode (fk_live_ key): these tools reach real people through your dedicated senders. They are for operating during development. {{mcp.runtime_rule}} Every send passes Flow's send gate (channel rules, budgets, pacing). Read before you write: list_conversations, get_conversation_messages, then reply. send_message and reply take a message that is one kind object, as in a webhook reply ({"kind": "basic", "text": "..."}; a string is a basic message's text); a kind, field or value the channel does not take answers unsupported_content with param and a hint saying what to send instead. edit_message and unsend_message change a message you sent within the channel's limits (Telegram, iMessage); past a limit they answer unsupported_content, so send a new message instead. Errors carry hint and doc_url; explain_error explains a type. whoami shows plans: the paid plans (Shared, Dedicated), the Outbound add-on, their prices and upgrade_url, where the workspace owner buys one; a full trial or plan hint ends with that link. To build and test an integration, connect with a test key (fk_test_) instead. {{mcp.owner_rule}}
