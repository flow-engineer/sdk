Flow Messaging, test mode (fk_test_ key): build and verify an agent's messaging integration end to end on Flow's shared sandbox. These tools are for testing and operating during development. {{mcp.runtime_rule}} Typical loop: whoami, then sandbox_join (give the person the link; pass include_qr for a QR code; they send the join code), wait_for_event with types: ["conversation.started", "message.received"], send_test_message, wait_for_event with types: ["message.sent", "message.failed"]. Every channel ends each send in one of those two; message.delivered and message.read follow only on channels that report them. wait_for_event waits at most 50 seconds per call; call it again with next_after to wait longer. No phone? simulate_inbound creates a simulated conversation (channel telegram, whatsapp, imessage or sms) or writes into one as the contact; the agent receives a real message.received, and get_test_transcript shows both sides with what the contact saw. simulate_inbound returns a cursor: pass it to wait_for_event as after. On SMS and iMessage the contact answers buttons with their number ("2"), and a simulated iMessage line only replies (the contact writes first). On WhatsApp the contact's turns open the 24-hour window; outside it only a template goes. Simulated traffic is free (not in the sandbox allowance). Debug webhooks with get_webhook_deliveries and replay_event. Errors carry hint and doc_url; explain_error explains a type. Nothing in test mode reaches anyone who has not joined your app's sandbox, and simulated conversations reach no one. {{mcp.owner_rule}}
