Flow's shared iMessage line is self-serve on sign-in at flow.engineer (or through the Flow CLI, `{{trial.cli_setup}}`): the first sign-in starts the account's free trial, and every app of the account then sees the line in `GET /v0/senders`. People join an app on it by sending the app's join message (the sender's `join_code` text, for example `Hi Sandbox app (ref wild-otter-04508705)`) to the line. The trial has room for {{trial.people_per_channel}} people per channel in total, over all the account's apps; the next person is held: their messages are kept, not delivered, and sends to them answer `403 permission` with `sandbox_contact_limit`, until you delete a contact you no longer need (`DELETE /v0/contacts/{contact_id}`). The Shared plan, bought in the dashboard ({{dashboard_url}}, {{plans.path}}), replaces the trial: 200 people a day and 300 a month per channel. The shared line is iMessage only, replies only (the person writes first) and ignores group chats. Writing first (`sender.imessage.can_initiate`) is available on dedicated lines only, with the Dedicated plan's Outbound add-on, bought in the same place.
