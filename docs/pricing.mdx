| | Free | Pro | Business | Enterprise |

| For | Building and trying Flow | A first agent in production | Teams running agents at scale | Large or regulated deployments |

| Senders | Shared sandbox | Coming soon | Coming soon | Custom |

Every plan covers WhatsApp, Telegram and iMessage with the same API. Message content and media are kept 30 days by default; retention is set per plan.

## What to know now

- **Test mode is free.** Everything in the [Quickstart](/quickstart) works on the Free plan.
- **WhatsApp's own fees are passed through at cost.** WhatsApp charges per template message by category (marketing, utility, authentication); Flow adds no markup to them.
- **Plan limits are enforced by the send gate**, as a monthly allowance of new conversations. Replies to people who wrote to you are what agents mostly send.
- **Billing** is in the [dashboard](https://api.flow.engineer/admin).

Questions about pricing or volume: write to us from the dashboard.
