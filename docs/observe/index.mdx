Flow Observe shows you what your agent says to people, and which replies went wrong. It works on any agent, built with any framework or none.

- **Every conversation**, message by message, with how long each reply took.
- **Every reply graded.** Exact rules run first, then a grading model. Each reply gets a verdict: `good`, `wrong`, `unhelpful` or `unsafe`.
- **Failures grouped into issues** by a fixed rule (the check that failed, the verdict and the topic), ranked by how many conversations they touch. Each issue links to the replies that show it and comes with a suggested fix you can copy into your prompt or code.
- **The inside of each turn**, from your agent's traces: model calls (model, tokens), tool calls, retrieval, errors and retries, and how long each step took.
- **Stats and usage**: conversations, replies, reply time (median and 95th percentile), and the conversation count you are billed on.

<Note>
Flow Observe is in **early access**. The Flow team creates your account and keys. Your account takes **test data only** until the Flow team enables it for production: send synthetic conversations, never real people's messages, until then.
</Note>

## Two ways in

Pick the one that matches how your agent talks to people.

| | On Flow Messaging | On any other channel |
|---|---|---|
| Your agent talks through | [Flow Messaging](/quickstart) (Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS) | Your own SMS provider, web chat, WhatsApp integration or anything else |
| What you add | Nothing. Optionally one tag on your traces | One OpenTelemetry trace per turn |
| Where conversations come from | Flow Messaging's event log | Your traces |
| The text graded | What Flow Messaging delivered and sent | What you report in `flow.user_message` and `flow.agent_reply` |
| The inside of each turn | When you also send traces | Yes, from the same traces |
| Test chats | [Simulated contacts](/concepts/test-and-live-mode#simulated-contacts-test-without-a-phone) today | `flow test` (coming, see [Test before you ship](/trust)) |

## Get access

Observe is in early access, and the Flow team turns it on for you. Write to the Flow team at samyak@flow.engineer or prabal@flow.engineer, and say whether your agent talks through Flow Messaging (give your app ID, `app_...`, from `GET /v1/app`) or through another channel (traces). The Flow team sets up your account and your agent, and tells you how you get your keys: a **trace key** (`otk_test_...`) for traces, and a **CI key** (`oci_...`) for [`flow test`](/trust). Never send a key by email or in a chat.

Keep a trace key in `FLOW_OBSERVE_TRACE_KEY` on your server, never in a browser or mobile app, and never in a chat with an AI assistant. Your test conversations then show in the dashboard at [flow.engineer/observe](https://flow.engineer/observe), labelled as test data.

## Test data and production

- Keys come in two modes, as in Flow Messaging: `otk_test_...` and `otk_live_...` for traces, `fk_test_...` and `fk_live_...` for Flow Messaging apps. Data never crosses between modes.
- Until the Flow team enables your account for production, Observe accepts test keys and test data only. A live key is refused with `403`.
- Test conversations are never billed.

## How your data is kept

- Message text and the content of your traces (prompts, tool inputs and results) are encrypted at rest with a key per account, and deleted when your account's retention period ends.
- Every time a person opens a conversation's text in the dashboard, it is logged.
- On other channels, the ids you send (conversation, contact, message) are stored only as keyed hashes, so a phone number used as an id is never stored as a phone number. On Flow Messaging, Observe keeps Flow's own ids (`conv_...`, `msg_...`, `ct_...`) and never the person's phone number or handle.
- The dashboard shows span details (names, models, tokens, timings, errors), not prompts or tool inputs.
- Dashboard pages load no analytics and no third-party scripts. Sign-in is with Google, limited to the people on your account's list.
