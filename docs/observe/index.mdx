Flow Observe shows you what your agent says to people, and which replies went wrong. It works on any agent, built with any framework or none.

- **Every conversation**, message by message, with how long each reply took.
- **Every reply graded.** Exact rules run first, then a grading model. Each reply gets a verdict: `good`, `wrong`, `unhelpful` or `unsafe`.
- **Failures grouped into issues** by a fixed rule (the check that failed, the verdict and the topic), ranked by how many conversations they touch. Each issue links to the replies that show it and comes with a suggested fix you can copy into your prompt or code.
- **The inside of each turn**, from your agent's traces: model calls (model, tokens), tool calls, retrieval, errors and retries, and how long each step took.
- **Stats and usage**: conversations, replies, reply time (median and 95th percentile), and the conversation count you are billed on.

<Note>
Flow Observe is in **early access**. You add your agent and make its keys yourself on Observe's Setup page. Your account takes **test data only** until the Flow team enables it for production: send synthetic conversations, never real people's messages, until then.
</Note>

## Two ways in

Pick the one that matches how your agent talks to people.

| | On Flow Messaging | On any other channel |
|---|---|---|
| Your agent talks through | [Flow Messaging](/quickstart) (Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS) | Your own SMS provider, web chat, WhatsApp integration or anything else |
| What you add | Nothing. Optionally one tag on your traces | One OpenTelemetry trace per turn |
| Where conversations come from | Flow Messaging's event log | Your traces |
| The text graded | What Flow Messaging delivered and sent | What you report in `flow.user_message` and `flow.agent_reply` |
| The inside of each turn | When you also send traces | Yes, from the same traces |
| Test chats | [Simulated contacts](/concepts/test-and-live-mode#simulated-contacts-test-without-a-phone) today | `flow test` (early access, see [Test before you ship](/trust)) |

## Works alongside your other tools

Observe takes standard OpenTelemetry over OTLP/HTTP. If you already send traces somewhere, add Observe as a second exporter: the same spans go to both, and nothing in your code is specific to Flow except the `flow.*` attributes. You can try Observe next to what you use today, and stop at any time by removing the exporter.

<CodeGroup>
```python Python
provider.add_span_processor(BatchSpanProcessor(OTLPSpanExporter(  # next to your existing processor
    endpoint="https://flow.engineer/observe/v1/traces",
    headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['FLOW_OBSERVE_TRACE_KEY']}"})))
```

```ts TypeScript
spanProcessors: [
  new BatchSpanProcessor(yourExistingExporter),
  new BatchSpanProcessor(new OTLPTraceExporter({ url: "https://flow.engineer/observe/v1/traces",
    headers: { Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.FLOW_OBSERVE_TRACE_KEY}` } })),
],
```
</CodeGroup>

The TypeScript lines go in your provider's constructor (OpenTelemetry JS 2.x). The imports and the rest of the setup are on [Send traces from any agent](/observe/traces#1-set-up-the-exporter).

## Get access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with your Flow login">
    Open [flow.engineer/observe](https://flow.engineer/observe) and sign in with the same Flow login you use on flow.engineer. Observe is in early access: if it isn't on for you yet, the page says so; ask the Flow team (samyak@flow.engineer or prabal@flow.engineer) to turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your agent">
    In the dashboard, add your agent. On Flow Messaging, choose Flow Messaging and paste a test key of your app (`fk_test_...`; a key made just for Observe is best). On any other channel, choose traces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your keys">
    For traces, make a **trace key** (`otk_test_...`); for `flow test` in CI, a **CI key** (`oci_...`). Each is shown once. Keep a trace key in `FLOW_OBSERVE_TRACE_KEY` on your server, never in a browser or mobile app, and never in a chat with an AI assistant. The dashboard shows your test conversations, labelled as test data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test data and production

- Keys come in two modes, as in Flow Messaging: `otk_test_...` and `otk_live_...` for traces, `fk_test_...` and `fk_live_...` for Flow Messaging apps. Data never crosses between modes.
- Until the Flow team enables your account for production, Observe accepts test keys and test data only. A live key is refused with `403`.
- Test conversations are never billed.

## How your data is kept

- Message text and the content of your traces (prompts, tool inputs and results) are encrypted at rest with a key per account. Both are deleted when your account's retention period ends.
- Every time a person opens a conversation's text in the dashboard, it is logged.
- On other channels, the ids you send (conversation, contact, message) are stored only as keyed hashes, so a phone number used as an id is never stored as a phone number. On Flow Messaging, Observe keeps Flow's own ids (`conv_...`, `msg_...`, `ct_...`) and never the person's phone number or handle.
- The dashboard shows span details (names, models, tokens, timings, errors), not prompts or tool inputs.
- Dashboard pages load no analytics and no third-party scripts. Sign-in is with your Flow login, limited to the people on your account's list.
