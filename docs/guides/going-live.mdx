Going live means sending from a **dedicated sender** of your own with a **live key**; your agent's code does not change. The examples below assume `FLOW_MESSAGING_KEY` holds your live key (`fk_live_...`).

```bash curl
export TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=...   # the token from @BotFather; never commit it

curl https://api.flow.engineer/v1/senders \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLOW_MESSAGING_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "{\"channel\": \"telegram\", \"telegram_bot_token\": \"$TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN\"}"
```

| Channel | How you go live today |
|---|---|
| Telegram | Self-serve: connect your own bot with a live key (below). |
| iMessage | Ask the Flow team: they connect a line to your app. Replies only. |
| WhatsApp | Ask the Flow team: they connect a number to your app, one Flow provides or one in your business's own WhatsApp Business Account. |

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a live key">
    Sign in to the [dashboard](https://api.flow.engineer/admin) with GitHub, switch to **Live**, and make an `fk_live_...` key on the [Keys page](https://api.flow.engineer/admin/keys?mode=live) (test keys take one call, see [Keys and sign-in](/get-a-key)). Keep it on your server only, in its own variable (for example `FLOW_MESSAGING_LIVE_KEY`) so it does not replace your test key. Live Telegram with your own bot is self-serve; iMessage lines and WhatsApp numbers are arranged with the Flow team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a dedicated sender">
    Telegram: `POST /v1/senders` with your bot's token; it is `active` at once. iMessage and WhatsApp: the Flow team connects a line or number to your app, and it then appears in `GET /v1/senders` with your live key (`POST /v1/senders` with `"channel": "imessage"` or `"whatsapp"` answers `501 not_implemented` with any key; build meanwhile on [simulated conversations](/concepts/test-and-live-mode) of that channel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your webhook with the live key">
    Webhook endpoints and their signing secrets belong to one mode: your test endpoint never receives live events. `POST /v1/webhook_endpoints` again with the live key and store the new `whsec_...` secret where your live deployment reads it; the test endpoint keeps its own secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch the key">
    Set `FLOW_MESSAGING_KEY` to the live key and deploy. Watch `message.failed` and `sender.status_changed` for the first days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## WhatsApp

WhatsApp numbers are connected by the Flow team, not by API: `POST /v1/senders` with `"channel": "whatsapp"` answers `501 not_implemented`, and the number appears in `GET /v1/senders` once it is connected. The Flow team also disconnects it (`DELETE /v1/senders/{sender_id}` answers `501 not_implemented`).

- **Numbers:** either a number Flow provides, or a number in your business's own WhatsApp Business Account.
- **Quality and tiers:** the sender shows WhatsApp's `quality_rating` (`green`, `yellow`, `red`) and its messaging tier in `limits.whatsapp_tier`. Replies to people who wrote first are not limited by the tier.
- **Fees:** on a number Flow provides, WhatsApp's own per-message fees (charged by Meta) are passed through. With your own WhatsApp Business Account, Meta bills its fees to your business directly, so add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager. See [Pricing](/pricing).

### WhatsApp templates

A template is a message WhatsApp approved in advance. On WhatsApp you need one to message someone first, or to write more than 24 hours after their last message. Templates are created on your WhatsApp number:

```bash curl
curl https://api.flow.engineer/v1/templates \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLOW_MESSAGING_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "sender": "snd_01JB8ZC3K5M7P9R1T3V5X7Z9B1",
    "name": "order_ready",
    "language": "en",
    "category": "utility",
    "components": [
      { "type": "body", "text": "Hi {{1}}, your {{2}} is ready for pickup." }
    ]
  }'
```

- Names use lowercase letters, digits and underscores. A name and language pair is unique per sender.
- `category` is `utility` (updates about something the person asked for), `marketing` or `authentication`; it sets WhatsApp's fee.
- A new template is `pending`. You receive `template.status_changed` when WhatsApp approves, rejects (with `rejection_reason`) or pauses it.
- Send it as `template` content with values for its placeholders:

```json
{ "content": { "type": "template", "template_id": "tpl_01JB8ZC3K5M7P9R1T3V5X7Z9B1", "language": "en", "params": { "body": ["Asha", "cake"] } } }
```

## iMessage: a line from the Flow team

- **Get one:** ask the Flow team. They connect a dedicated line to your app; it is listed in `GET /v1/senders?channel=imessage` with your live key.
- **Replies only:** the person always writes first. Starting a conversation with a new contact is refused with `403 permission`.
- **Get people to write:** the sender's `address.link` is the line's opt-in link (when it has one): it opens Messages with the line and a prefilled text. Publish it on your site, receipts or QR codes. See [Build an iMessage agent](/guides/imessage-agent).

## Telegram: your own bot

Create a bot with **@BotFather** and send its token: `POST /v1/senders` with `channel: "telegram"` and `telegram_bot_token`. It is connected and `active` at once. See [Build a Telegram agent](/guides/telegram-agent#2-your-own-telegram-bot).

- **New token:** after revoking a token in @BotFather, send the new one the same way. The bot stays the same sender; one Telegram rejected meanwhile is `flagged` until you do. Messages queued while it is flagged wait at most 72 hours, then fail with `outside_window` (`channel_code` `queued_too_long`).
- **Disconnect:** `DELETE /v1/senders/{sender_id}` removes the bot's webhook and token and retires the sender.
- **One app per bot:** connecting a bot to another app moves it there and retires the old sender.

## Before you launch

- Handle `outside_window`, `new_contact_limit` and `sender_throttled` (see [Errors](/errors/outside_window)). Know the [sending limits and abuse signals](/concepts/send-gate#rule-3-abuse-protection): the same first message to many new contacts throttles a sender for 24 hours.
- Deduplicate webhook deliveries on the event `id`, and use event IDs as idempotency keys.
- Subscribe your webhook only to the event types you use.
- Message content and media are kept for your plan's retention period (30 days by default); store anything you need longer yourself.
